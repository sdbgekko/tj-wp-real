About TJ's

Serving Our Guests for Over 38 Years

No longer the best kept secret on the Gulf Beaches... TJ's Italian Cafe has become the favorite dining spot of locals and visitors alike. Locals take advantage of visiting TJ's often during the offseason.

Beachy and casual, yet elegant and tasteful, you can dine inside or enjoy a cool gulf breeze from the palm-laden deck outdoors. Owners Thomas John Smith (Chef TJ) and Kim Grady-Smith are warm and inviting hosts.

Chef TJ personally oversees every dish that leaves his kitchen — from specialty brick-oven pizzas to perfect handmade pastas, every bite reflects 38 years of Gulf Coast Italian tradition.
